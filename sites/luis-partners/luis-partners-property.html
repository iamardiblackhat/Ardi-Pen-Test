Selling with Dee

Owners instruct her to sell. She agrees a price against what is completing nearby, prepares the particulars, negotiates the offers, and stays with the sale through to exchange.

  • Sellers

    A pricing conversation, then a sales instruction. Not a brochure for its own sake.

  • Buyers

    Start from the portfolio. If a home fits, ask for the particulars. If it does not, ask what else is coming.

  • Where she sells

    Hampstead, Belsize Park, Kingsbury and the rest of north-west London, plus prime London when the instruction is there.
